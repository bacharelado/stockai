# StockAI — Product Roadmap

The roadmap is intentionally separated into product, operations and intelligence workstreams so production fixes can remain small and reviewable.

## Platform

- Super Admin with global company visibility
- Plan limits enforced automatically
- Billing and subscriptions
- Invitations and account recovery
- Transactional notifications
- Stronger observability and operational metrics

## Operations

- Stock transfer between branches
- Supplier-product relationships and purchase orders
- Barcode scanning workflows
- Sales / POS
- Customers and orders
- Operational finance features

## Intelligence

- Demand forecasting
- Days-to-stockout estimation
- Smart replenishment recommendations
- Detection of stagnant inventory
- Seasonality analysis
- Profitability insights
- Natural-language business assistant

## Engineering priorities

Before larger commercial modules are introduced, the project should continue strengthening transactional integrity, PostgreSQL production behavior, automated testing and observability.
